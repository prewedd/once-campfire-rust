# Workspace redesign

Campfire's own look is DHH's: big bubbles, generous space, room list on the right. PreWedd's team
lives in Slack and Discord, so this redesign gives them the chat they know, a little better, in
PreWedd's UI kit (zinc neutrals, rose `#F63D68` for what's ours or needs you).

## What changed

- **Type.** Inter, self-hosted (Latin and Cyrillic, variable weight), at Slack's sizes: 15px/22px
  messages, bold 15px names, 12px muted times.
- **Frame.** The sidebar on the left, ink-dark with a little rose light: the workspace's name, then
  Channels (the current one in rose, unread ones bold with a dot) and Direct messages (a compact list
  with avatars, a + for a new one), you and settings along the bottom. A header bar over the
  conversation: "# General".
- **Messages.** Compact, flat rows; a run of messages from one person keeps one name and avatar, and
  each follow-up's time shows in the gutter on hover. Everyone on the left, you included. Mentions of
  you get a rose wash and bar; @mentions are chips.
- **Hover toolbar.** Floating over the row's top right: 👍 ❤️ 😂, add reaction (the emoji picker),
  reply in thread, and "…" for the rest.
- **Reactions.** One pill per emoji with a count, rose when one is yours; click a pill to add or
  take back your reaction; its tooltip names who reacted.
- **Emoji.** A picker (search, categories, recently used, keyboard: arrows, Enter, Escape) in the
  composer, in thread replies, and for reactions; and `:shortcode` suggestions as you type, with
  Slack's shortcodes.
- **Composer.** One rounded box with its tools inside, "Message #General" (or "Reply…" in a
  thread), rose Send.
- **Threads.** The summary shows the latest repliers, the count and "View thread ›" on hover; the
  thread view heads with the parent and a "Replies" rule.
- **Themes.** A palette button by Settings picks the mode (System, Light, Dark) and a theme:
  Graphite (the default), Aubergine, Ocean, Forest, Violet, Rose or Mono (a light sidebar). Each is
  a sidebar and an accent; everything else follows the mode. Saved per device, applied before the
  page paints.
- **Presence.** A green dot for whoever has Campfire open and visible, a hollow one for away (all
  their pages hidden two minutes) or offline: on people in the sidebar and on profiles ("Active",
  "Away", "Offline"). `OnlineChannel` keeps it in memory; a person goes offline a few seconds after
  their last page closes, so moving between pages doesn't flicker.
- **The right-hand panel.** Threads and profiles open beside the conversation, as in Slack: the
  room stays put, the thread's reply box takes the focus, Escape or ✕ closes it, and "Open in full"
  goes to the thread's own page. The server renders the panel's version (with ids of its own)
  when Turbo asks for the `flexpane` frame.
- Light and dark, phones included.

## How it's built

- `crates/assets/overrides/workspace.css`: the design, as tokens then components. It sorts after
  every other stylesheet, so it restyles them in place; the account's custom styles still come last.
- `fonts/inter-*.woff2`: Inter 4 from Google Fonts' subsets (SIL Open Font License).
- `lib/emoji/picker.js` and `controllers/emoji_controller.js`: the picker and the buttons that open
  it. The emoji (`emoji/data.json`) are fetched the first time it opens; `emoji/prompt.html` feeds
  Lexxy's `:` prompt. Both are generated by `crates/assets/script/generate_emoji.py` from
  emojibase-data 15.3.2 (Emoji 14 and earlier, so they render everywhere).
- `initializers/workspace.js`: groups reactions into pills, toggles yours, and marks the current
  room in the sidebar, page-wide, without markup of its own.
- Templates add the toolbar's reactions, the composer's emoji button, prompt and placeholder, and the
  sidebar's header. The parity goldens leave these out (`crates/views/tests/messages_support`,
  `parity_a.rs`); `workspace_views.rs` covers them.
- The importmap now pins modules the overrides add to a pinned directory, as Rails would.
- `controllers/theme_controller.js` and `users/sidebars/_theme_picker.html` (the picker), a boot
  script in `layouts/application.html`; `channels/online.rs`; `threads/pane.html`,
  `users/pane.html` and `users/_profile.html` (the panel), opened by `initializers/workspace.js`.

## Slack-style surfaces (2026-10-09)

- **Settings as modals** (`lib/workspace/modal.js`): settings, room settings, new room/DM and the
  other card pages open in a `<dialog>` over the conversation, showing the page's own content;
  their forms are submitted from the modal, a redirect to another such page stays in it, anything
  else navigates. Closing after a change refreshes the page behind.
- **Notifications menu** (`lib/workspace/involvement.js`): the bell opens named choices with what
  each does; every icon-only button gets a tooltip from its screen-reader label.
- **Profiles in the panel from anywhere**, laid out as Slack's: photo, name, presence, bio,
  Message, Huddle (when you have a DM), contact details for administrators, Edit profile for yours.
- **Channel order** (`lib/workspace/reorder.js`): drag channels, or Alt+↑/↓, saved per person.
- **One heartbeat per page** (`controllers/refresh_room_controller.js`): a thread in the panel
  used to subscribe to `HeartbeatChannel` a second time, never got confirmed, and after 5 seconds
  disabled every composer on the page as "offline".

## Settings pages, Slack's way (2026-10-10)

Campfire's settings were centered cards of icon-only buttons, globe "translate" buttons and dotted
leaders. They now read as Slack's, with shared classes in `workspace.css` (`.ws-settings`,
`.ws-section`, `.ws-field`, `.ws-input`, `.ws-btn`, `.ws-rows`/`.ws-row`, `.ws-choice`):

- **Edit your profile** (`users/profiles/show.html`): Full name, Email, About me and New password
  as labelled fields, the photo on the right with Upload photo / Remove photo, Save changes; then
  Notifications (each channel with its setting by name, opening the notifications menu), Sign in on
  another device (Copy link, QR code), installing the app, and Sign out.
- **Workspace settings** (`accounts/edit.html`): the icon and name, Invite people (Copy link, QR
  code, Reset link), "Only admins can create channels", Members (email for admins, an Admin badge,
  Make admin / Remove admin, Remove), Integrations (Bots, Custom styles).
- **Create a channel / Channel settings** (`rooms/layouts/_form.html`, `opens`/`closeds`): a
  `#`-prefixed name, Visibility as Public / Private choices (they still switch between the open
  and closed forms), members as a checklist with a filter for private channels, Create channel /
  Save changes, and Delete this channel. A name typed in the modal survives the switch.
- Fixes: a modal form that redirects elsewhere (creating a channel) goes there instead of being
  overridden by the refresh of the page behind; the notifications menu opens inside a modal rather
  than behind it.
- `e2e/suites/channels.e2e.js` covers them; `e2e/tour/run` screenshots every main screen (desktop,
  dark, phone) into `e2e/out/tour-*.png` for review.

## Sidebar and header, Slack's way (2026-10-10)

- **Folding sections** (`lib/workspace/sidebar.js`): "Channels" and "Direct messages" are buttons
  that fold their section, remembered per device; folded, the room you're in and unread ones stay.
- **Private channels** show a lock instead of "#" (sidebar and the profile's notification list).
- **Workspace menu**: the workspace's name opens Invite people, Create a channel (when you may),
  Workspace settings and Your profile, each in the modal.
- **Header**: the channel's name (with ▾) opens its settings; huddle, notifications and ⋯ sit on the
  right; phones get a visible menu button.
- **Search** (`searches/index.html`): a "Search <workspace>" pill in every room's header opens it;
  the box sits in the header, results head with "Results for “…”" and a count, each labelled
  "in #channel"; with no query, recent searches as a list. The composer's magnifier is gone, and
  the normal sidebar stays.
- **New message** (`rooms/directs/new.html`): the + by Direct messages opens it in the modal: "To:"
  with the people autocomplete, then Start. **Conversation settings** list who's in it and offer
  "Delete conversation". Labels and tooltips say "channel" and "conversation", not "room" and "Ping".
- **Jump to** (Ctrl/⌘+K, `lib/workspace/sidebar.js`): channels (🔒 for private) and people from the sidebar, filtered by name as you type (names that start with it first), ↑↓ and Enter.
- **Bots and Custom styles** (`accounts/bots`, `accounts/custom_styles`): "Add a bot", each bot with its per-channel commands (Copy message command / Copy file command), the bot form as the profile form (Picture, Name, Webhook URL with what it does), Reset key and Remove bot as labelled sections; custom CSS as a labelled code field with Save changes.
- **Channel topics**: `rooms.topic` (nullable, added on boot; `schema::ADDED_COLUMNS`), set in
  Create a channel / Channel settings (trimmed, 250 characters at most, blank clears it; a form
  without the field leaves it), shown beside the name in the header on wider screens.
- **Message "⋯" menu**: a list of named actions (Reply in thread, Quote in reply, Copy link, Edit message, Download/Share for files) from each button's title; its emoji row stays only on phones, where there is no hover toolbar.
- **Custom status** (`user_statuses`, a table of this app's own created on boot;
  `users/statuses.rs`, `lib/workspace/status.js`): an emoji and up to 100 characters, set at the top
  of your profile (suggestions: In a meeting, Commuting, Out sick, Vacationing, Working remotely;
  "Clear after" 30 minutes to This week, the last two in your own time zone) or cleared. A status
  past its time reads as none. Pages read everyone's from `/users/statuses.json` (at most once a
  minute) and show the emoji after names on messages and conversations, a badge on your own
  picture, and a line on profiles, without changing the server's (cached) markup. The workspace
  menu offers "Update your status".
- **Browse channels** (`/channels`, `controllers/channels.rs`, `rooms/browse.html`): your channels
  by name with members, topic and a filter; Leave sets your involvement to "invisible" (out of the
  sidebar, no notifications) and Join sets it back to "mentions", with the bell's broadcasts. It's
  in the workspace menu and in "Add channel", which now offers Create a new channel / Browse channels.
- **Saved for later** (`saved_messages`, a table of this app's own; `controllers/saved.rs`,
  `lib/workspace/saved.js`, `searches/saved.html`): "Save for later" in a message's ⋯ menu (it reads
  "Remove from saved" once saved, from `/saved/ids`, since message HTML is cached and shared), a
  toast to confirm, and "Saved" under Threads in the sidebar, listing them newest first with their
  channels. Only messages you can still reach are saved or listed.
- **Pinned messages** (`pinned_messages`, a table of this app's own; `controllers/pins.rs`,
  `lib/workspace/pins.js`, `searches/pinned.html`): any member pins from a message's ⋯ menu ("Pin to
  channel" / "Unpin from channel"); pinned messages get a "📌 Pinned" label and a pale yellow wash;
  the header shows "📌 N", which opens the channel's pins in the modal. Refreshed on load, every 30
  seconds, and after a change. Painting writes only what changed (the header is watched, so writing
  it unconditionally looped).
- Huddles: the mini-window is wider so its controls fit; the "started a huddle" invite sits under
  the header (it covered the header's huddle button once that moved right).
- **Header members**: "👥 N" (active members) beside a channel's name opens its settings.
  **Alt+↑ / Alt+↓** go to the previous / next channel or conversation, in the sidebar's order.
- **Sign in and Join** (`sessions/new.html`, `users/new.html`): a card with the workspace icon, "Sign in to …" / "Join …", labelled Email and Password (and Full name, an optional photo), a full-width primary button, "Already have an account? Sign in", and "Trouble signing in? Email …" in place of the lanyard badge and icon buttons.
